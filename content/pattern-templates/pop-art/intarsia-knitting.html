Chart logic

One chart square equals one knitted stitch. Read right-side rows right-to-left and wrong-side rows left-to-right unless working in the round.

Pop Art adaptation: do not feed the full cross-stitch matrix directly into intarsia. The generator should reduce colors, remove one-stitch islands and correct the artwork for knit stitch aspect ratio.
